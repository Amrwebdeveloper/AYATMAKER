Put your own background clips here (mp4, mov, webm, mkv; subfolders are scanned).
Any size, orientation or fps works: clips are converted to 1080x1920 30fps (cover + center crop), audio removed,
and cached under cache/mine/. Clips shorter than 2 seconds or unreadable files are skipped with a warning.
Use --clips {pexels,mine,mixed} on make/batch. Default: mixed if this folder has clips, else pexels.
To skip a clip, add its file name (or a glob) to ../exclude.txt.
